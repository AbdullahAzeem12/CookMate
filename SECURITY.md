---

## Reporting a Vulnerability

The CookMate team takes the security of its application, local cryptographic vault, and user data seriously. If you discover a security vulnerability or sensitive information leakage, please report it responsibly.

### How to Report
- **Do not open a public issue.**
- Submit your finding privately via [GitHub Security Advisories](https://github.com/AbdullahAzeem12/CookMate/security/advisories/new) or directly contact the project maintainer via the repository profile.
- Provide a detailed description of the vulnerability, including:
  - Affected components, files, or dependency versions
  - Step-by-step reproduction instructions or a minimal proof of concept (PoC)
  - Potential impact on users, encryption keys, or data confidentiality

### Response Timeline
- **Acknowledgment:** Within 48 hours of receipt.
- **Triage & Assessment:** Within 5 business days.
- **Fix & Public Advisory:** Once a patch is developed and verified across supported versions, a coordinated release and advisory will be published.

Thank you for helping keep CookMate and its community safe!
